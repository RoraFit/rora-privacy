Support

Contact us

Email support@rora.fit with questions, feedback or problems. You can also send feedback from the app: Profile → Send Feedback.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent and removes your profile, plans, workout history and chat history.

If you can't use the app, email support@rora.fit from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Privacy

How Rora handles your data, including what is shared with Anthropic, is in the privacy policy.

Health and safety

Rora isn't medical advice. It uses AI, can make mistakes, and doesn't replace a doctor or other qualified professional. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts.

If you're struggling, call or text 988. In an emergency, call 911.

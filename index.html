Rora

Your AI personal trainer

Rora builds a workout plan around your goals, schedule, experience and equipment, and adjusts it as you train.

Now in private beta on iPhone.

How it works

  1. Tell Rora about yourself

    Your goals, the days you can train, your experience and the equipment you have.

  2. Get a plan built for you

    A week-by-week program that fits your schedule and what you have to train with.

  3. Train and adapt

    Train, log your sets and watch your plan adapt as you go.

What Rora does

  • A personalized plan

    Built around your goals, schedule, experience and equipment.

  • Chat with your trainer

    Ask questions, or ask Rora to adjust your plan.

  • Set and weight logging

    Tick off each set and record the weight you used.

  • Rest timer

    A rest timer between sets.

  • Milestones

    Milestones to mark your training along the way.

Safety and privacy

Rora is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new program.

You must be 18 or older to use Rora.

Rora never sells your data or uses it for ads. Read the privacy policy.
